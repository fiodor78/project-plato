# EPISTLES METADATA 0001 — addressees of Letters I–XIII

**Status:** Source-verified Phase 1 metadata note

## Purpose

Verify the traditional manuscript numbering and addressee mapping of the thirteen Platonic Epistles before assigning work-specific authenticity grades.

This note concerns **identity and address**, not authorship.

## Sources

1. Diogenes Laertius III.61: the Epistles are thirteen in number and are grouped by addressee as one to Aristodemus, two to Archytas, four to Dionysius, one to Hermias/Erastus/Coriscus, one each to Leodamas, Dion and Perdiccas, and two to Dion's friends.
2. R. G. Bury's Loeb edition/translation: individual manuscript-order Epistle headings and prefatory notes.

## Important variant: Letter X

Diogenes' grouped list gives **Aristodemus**, whereas the transmitted Letter X heading and modern editions give **Aristodorus**.

PROJECT PLATO preserves this discrepancy rather than silently normalizing it away:

- normalized text-level addressee: Aristodorus;
- variant ancient attestation: Aristodemus;
- review status: SOURCE_VERIFIED_WITH_VARIANT.

## Methodological consequence

Epistolary identity metadata is independent from authenticity.

The project therefore does **not** infer that a letter is genuine merely because its addressee and manuscript position are well attested.
