# APPENDIX PLATONICA 0002 — Definitions, On Justice, On Virtue

**Status:** Phase 1 provisional authenticity assessment

This tranche closes three additional high-confidence pseudo-Platonic cases.

## Definitions — D

Modern scholarship does not treat the extant *Definitions* as a work by Plato. Recent scholarship explicitly calls the collection pseudo-Platonic and emphasizes its composite/late philosophical strata.

## On Justice — D

*On Justice* belongs to the Appendix Platonica. Current specialist work treats it as an apocryphal/pseudo-Platonic Socratic dialogue while still recognizing its importance for the history of Socratic and Academic literature.

## On Virtue — D

*On Virtue* is treated as pseudo-Platonic and is studied as a pseudepigraphic reworking of Platonic material, especially the *Meno*.

## Project consequence

All three works remain in the historical attribution catalog. Classification D affects the CUSTODIAN-side authorship assessment only; it does not erase their transmission history and is not automatically exposed to PLATO.
