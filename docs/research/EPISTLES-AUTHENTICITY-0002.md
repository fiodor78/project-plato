# EPISTLES AUTHENTICITY 0002 — completion of the canonical thirteen

**Status:** Phase 1 provisional assessment tranche

## Purpose

Complete work-specific provisional A/B/C/D assessments for all thirteen canonical Platonic Epistles without treating the collection as a single authorship block.

This tranche covers II, III, IV, V, VI, IX, X, XI and XIII. Earlier assertions already covered I, VII, VIII and XII.

## Evidence model

The classifications deliberately distinguish:

- broad or repeated rejection in critical scholarship;
- letters with a meaningful defense tradition;
- current or recent scholarship that reopens the collection as a deliberately unified Platonic literary work;
- work-specific arguments from chronology, diction, literary dependence, philosophical content and biographical plausibility.

No letter receives a grade merely because it is transmitted in the canonical thirteen.

## Methodological note on Helfer (2023)

Helfer's book is important because it prevents PROJECT PLATO from flattening recent scholarship into a false consensus of rejection. It argues for the literary unity of the thirteen letters and reads the collection as a deliberate Platonic philosophical work.

That does not automatically turn every minor letter into grade B. Where the negative authorship case is much stronger and the positive tradition relatively thin, the project uses C.

## Consequence

The traditional thirteen-letter collection now has complete **provisional** work-specific authenticity coverage.

These grades remain CUSTODIAN-only and do not automatically determine what enters PLATO's ORIGINAL runtime corpus.
