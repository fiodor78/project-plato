# THRASYLLAN DUBIA 0001 — first modern-authenticity tranche

**Status:** Phase 1 provisional assessment note

This tranche separates **live disputes (B)** from works **substantially rejected by modern scholarship (C)** while preserving their historical inclusion in the Thrasyllan canon.

## B — disputed

- **Alcibiades I** — substantial modern disagreement remains.
- **Hippias Major** — the case remains explicitly unresolved in current reference scholarship.
- **Clitophon** — long disputed; modern specialist work includes serious defenses of Platonic authorship.

These are not treated as “probably spurious” merely because doubt exists.

## C — probably inauthentic

- **Alcibiades II**
- **Hipparchus**
- **Rival Lovers / Amatores**
- **Theages**
- **Minos**
- **Epinomis**

These works remain historically important because they were included in the ancient canonical transmission, but modern scholarship substantially favors non-Platonic authorship.

## Methodological point

C and D are intentionally different:

- **C** commonly captures historically canonical works now judged probably non-Platonic.
- **D** is reserved for material whose pseudo-Platonic status is especially strong, including extra-canonical spuria and overwhelmingly rejected cases.

The grades remain CUSTODIAN-only and do not themselves become knowledge of PLATO.
